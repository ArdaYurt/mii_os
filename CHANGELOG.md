# Mii_OS versions

Each version below is a GitHub release (tag `vX.Y.Z`). The box shows the newest one's notes in its
update pop-up. To make a new version, raise the number in [`VERSION`](VERSION) and add its notes
here, at the top, in the same pull request: the first bullets are what the pop-up shows, so put the
biggest change first. Pull requests that don't change `VERSION` still reach the box as builds of the
current version.

## 0.4.0

- Menu music, with music and effects volume in Settings › Sounds
- A pointing hand and Wii-style page arrows
- Options on every channel: move it, make folders, app info
- Settings › HOME Menu, to switch the HOME Menu on
- Screen Share channel for iPhone and iPad (AirScreen)
- Your own logos and sounds from the laptop
- Fixed: the update pop-up looping on "Install unknown apps"
- Version numbers, with these notes in the update pop-up

## 0.3.0

- Wii-like sounds, and your own sound pack
- The HOME Menu when you press Home inside an app
- Move channels and make named folders
- Built-in updates, from the pop-up or Settings › Mii_OS Update
- Sharper 4K channel art, and a fix for the channel screen closing by itself

## 0.2.0

- The Wii look: white channels, curved dock, clock
- The TV channel, for your own IPTV playlist
- Stremio channel and the Mii_OS icon set
- Speed-up tool for the box
- Works on Android 11 boxes

# Mii_OS versions

Each version below is a GitHub release (tag `vX.Y.Z`). The box shows the newest one's notes in its
update pop-up. To make a new version, raise the number in [`VERSION`](VERSION) and add its notes
here, at the top, in the same pull request: the first bullets are what the pop-up shows, so put the
biggest change first. Pull requests that don't change `VERSION` still reach the box as builds of the
current version.

## 0.6.3

- Nuvio, an open-source alternative to Stremio that's lighter on the box, has its own channel next to Stremio's: open it and press Install to get it from the Play Store. It plays the same add-ons; add them in Nuvio's Settings › Addons

## 0.6.2

- The remote's Netflix, YouTube and Prime Video buttons work again with Mii_OS as home, and can be set to open something else in Settings › Remote buttons

## 0.6.1

- Plezy, a faster open-source Plex app, has its own channel: open it and press Install. It comes from its developer's GitHub, with no ads, and plays your films in 4K, HDR and Dolby Vision

## 0.6.0

- Remote buttons: the remote's TV+ button can open the TV channel, Plex or any channel you like. Set it in Settings › Remote buttons
- If a button isn't recognised, press OK on its row and then the button: Mii_OS learns it
- The Assistant button can be set too, on remotes that send it as a button (the Mi Box S remote doesn't, so it stays Google Assistant)

## 0.5.2

- A download bar for installs and Mii_OS updates: how far the download is, then installing, then done, or why it didn't work
- Fixed: Screen Share (and other channels installed from Mii_OS) kept saying Install after the app was installed

## 0.5.1

- Going home from the HOME Menu: the home screen comes out of a soft white glow and settles into place for exactly as long as the home-exit sound, so the two end together

## 0.5.0

- Screen Share now uses an open-source AirPlay receiver from GitHub: no ads, no Play Store. Open Screen Share and press Install
- Screen Share keeps its place and folder on the home screen; you can uninstall AirScreen

## 0.4.3

- The home screen looks more like the Wii Menu: plain white, empty places in soft grey with fine lines and a faint "Mii", a grey bottom bar edged in blue, and a segmented clock
- Branded channel screens: Spotify opens on green, YouTube on red, with the channel's name drifting behind and its logo floating in front
- A new pointing hand, closer to the Wii's
- New Settings and Share buttons in the dock, glossy Wii blue
- A sound for the remote's volume buttons that slides smoothly when you hold or repeat them (volume in your sound pack; switch it off in Settings › Sounds)

## 0.4.2

- Channels fill their tiles edge to edge: no grey frame, and the blue focus ring sits right on the logo
- Put channels in any place, empty places included, like an Android home screen
- Sharper logos: your images are scaled once to the tile's exact size

## 0.4.1

- HOME Menu: its own sound for going home from an app (home-exit in your sound pack)

## 0.4.0

- Menu music, with music and effects volume in Settings › Sounds
- A pointing hand and Wii-style page arrows
- Options on every channel: move it, make folders, app info
- Settings › HOME Menu, to switch the HOME Menu on (works on Google TV's settings too)
- Screen Share channel for iPhone and iPad (AirScreen)
- Your own logos, sounds, font and pointer, copied from the laptop
- HOME Menu: the app stays visible behind it, blurred and dimmed; bigger, clearer buttons
- Fixed: the update pop-up looping on "Install unknown apps"
- Version numbers, with these notes in the update pop-up

## 0.3.0

- Wii-like sounds, and your own sound pack
- The HOME Menu when you press Home inside an app
- Move channels and make named folders
- Built-in updates, from the pop-up or Settings › Mii_OS Update
- Sharper 4K channel art, and a fix for the channel screen closing by itself

## 0.2.0

- The Wii look: white channels, curved dock, clock
- The TV channel, for your own IPTV playlist
- Stremio channel and the Mii_OS icon set
- Speed-up tool for the box
- Works on Android 11 boxes
